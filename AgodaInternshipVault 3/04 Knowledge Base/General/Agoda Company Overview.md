## For Internship Report — Company History (prose)

Agoda was founded in 2005 by Michael Kenny and Robert Rosenstein, two long-time friends who had previously run PlanetHoliday.com, a Thailand-based hotel booking site focused on North American and European travelers. After a major Google search-ranking algorithm change severely impacted that business, the pair pivoted and launched Agoda as an Asia-based online travel company built to compete with larger Western players. Agoda.com went live in Singapore with 7,000 hotels in Asia and 33,000 worldwide.

In 2007, Agoda was acquired by Booking Holdings (then known as Priceline Group), which provided the capital and infrastructure to accelerate its growth. Michael Kenny retired in 2010, and Robert Rosenstein became CEO. Over the following years, the company scaled rapidly — surpassing 200,000 hotel listings by 2012 — and expanded its capabilities through the 2014 acquisition of Qlika, an Israeli micro-marketing startup whose team remains with Agoda today.

Agoda broadened beyond hotels in the late 2010s: Homes (private accommodations) launched in 2017, followed by Flights in 2019, marking the company's evolution from a hotel-booking site into a broader travel marketplace. Also in 2019, Rocket Travel merged with Agoda to form Rocket Travel by Agoda, extending its reach through B2B and white-label partnerships. John Brown, who had joined as Chief Product Officer in 2012, was appointed CEO in 2018.

By 2021, Agoda's inventory had grown to 2.9 million hotels and homes across 56,000 cities, and the company launched Activities, adding tours and experiences to its offering. Omri Barzilay was appointed CEO in 2022, the same year the company refreshed its core Values. In 2024, Agoda introduced its formal purpose statement, "Bridging the World Through Travel."

Today, Agoda is one of Asia's leading travel technology companies, headquartered in Singapore with a Global Operations Center in Bangkok and offices in more than 50 locations worldwide. Its platform now spans over 6 million hotels and holiday properties, more than 130,000 flight routes, and over 300,000 activities and experiences — reflecting its growth from a small Southeast Asian startup into a global travel marketplace within the Booking Holdings group.

## For Internship Report — Company Vision (prose)

By 2030, Agoda aims to be the preferred Travel Marketplace in the Asia-Pacific (APAC) region — extending beyond accommodation into flights, activities, ground transport, and every other purchase a traveler makes from the moment they start planning a trip until they return. Agoda positions itself as a deal master with the best price competitiveness, a localization expert leveraging its deep understanding of Asian and Western markets, the leading B2B platform in the space, and a worldwide challenger to larger competitors on behalf of its parent company, Booking Holdings Inc. This vision is underpinned by Agoda's stated purpose, introduced in 2024: "Bridging the World Through Travel."
