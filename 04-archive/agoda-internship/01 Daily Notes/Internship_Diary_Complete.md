# Internship Diary: May 22 – July 31, 2026
*IT Support Intern — Agoda, One Bangkok L29*

---

## Week 1: Onboarding & Training (May 22–29, 2026)

**22/05/2026 (Fri) — First Day**

I walked into One Bangkok L29 on a Friday morning not entirely sure what to expect. The Summer Internship 2026 cohort gathered for a Laptop Collection & System Training session — my first real look at how Agoda's IT support operation runs. By the end of the day I had my badge, my equipment, and my first assignment: prepare 31 Dell Precision 5570 devices for CSR deployment using a specific MDT server. It was a bigger project than I anticipated for a first day, but that was exactly the kind of challenge I came here for.

**25/05/2026 (Mon) — Orientation Day**

The official onboarding session introduced us to IT support team leadership and filled in the full picture of what the next ten weeks would look like. My device build project was confirmed — 31 devices, target completion early July. I left orientation with a clear goal and a long task list.

**26/05/2026 (Tue) — Intensive Training Day 1**

The first of four consecutive training days covered more ground than I expected. Five sessions back to back: an overview of Agoda IT operations; the TARS ticketing workflow (handle, create, escalate); the L2 support dashboard; a deep dive into TARS and KACE asset management; and Printix printer management. By the end of the day my notebook was full and my head wasn't far behind.

**27/05/2026 (Wed) — Intensive Training Day 2**

Two focused sessions: Microsoft Admin tools (Azure AD, Intune Admin Center, Okta, AD management), followed by Mac Build using JAMF — enrollment methods, common Mac issues, and the troubleshooting runbook. The breadth of the stack surprised me; I had expected more Windows-centric work.

**28/05/2026 (Thu) — Intensive Training Day 3**

Windows laptop build via Autopilot provisioning, then TV and display management — building a PC for a video wall, configuring MagicSign software, troubleshooting display issues. That second session planted an idea that would come back to me later in the internship.

**29/05/2026 (Fri) — Intensive Training Day 4**

The final training session covered general laptop and hardware troubleshooting: network certificate issues, hardware diagnostics, CEG troubleshooting. An end-of-week review confirmed we had touched every core IT support domain. I felt ready to start.

## Week 2: Device Preparation Begins (June 1–5, 2026)

**02/06/2026 (Tue)**

Thirty-one Dell Precision 5570s arrived. I spent the morning verifying every serial number against the inventory list, then set up a dedicated cleaning and reimaging station with MDT server access. The workflow I settled on — clean hardware, secure-erase storage, reimage with CSR Windows from MDT, update KACE — would become muscle memory over the next two weeks. By end of day I had 8 devices moving through the pipeline.

**05/06/2026 (Fri)**

End of week two. Twenty-seven of 31 devices complete (87%), all KACE records updated. The final 4 were entering verification. The repetition was humbling — there is no shortcut to doing this correctly, and I learned to slow down on the verification step rather than rush it.

**#515878** — Thanaprachanon, Pakorn requested a switch to a 32" 4K monitor (P1-Urgent). Replaced and configured. One of the last monitor upgrades I would handle this internship.

**30/07/2026 (Thu)**

**#516394** — Slack-requested support from Saravanja, Matija (P3-Medium). Resolved. Then the most satisfying thing I did all internship: submitted the **WallKeeper** project.

WallKeeper is a PowerShell script I developed to solve a recurring operational problem. The Grafana video wall NUC machines running MagicSign needed periodic restarts to stay stable, and it was being done manually. The script automates the restart on a biweekly schedule — Windows Task Scheduler, every other Friday at 12:00 PM — and logs every run to `C:\VideoWallLogs\restart_log.txt`. It was a small independent project, but it was mine: something I identified, built, and delivered. That felt different from closing a ticket.

**31/07/2026 (Fri) — Offboarding Day**

The 2026 Summer Internship Program came to a close with Offboarding Day. The morning started with the Paper Ranger volunteering activity, run in partnership with Agoda's Sustainability team — a chance to give something back as a cohort before parting ways. The rest of the day was lighter: a photo booth, informal networking across the intern group, Agoji Lab activities, and prize giveaways. It was loud and warm and a little bittersweet.

Ten weeks. I came in not knowing what a real IT support operation looked like at scale, and I left having prepared 31 devices for CSR deployment, installed 30 monitors with a team across an entire office building, closed 20+ support tickets, onboarded dozens of new hires, and shipped a script that will keep running long after I was gone. The team treated me like a contributor, not a visitor. I am grateful for that.
