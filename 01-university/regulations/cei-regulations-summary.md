# KMITL University Rules & Regulations Summary

> Generated by NotebookLM from: Undergraduate Regulations B.E. 2022, Computer Engineering (International) curriculum, and Thai institute regulations (พ.ศ. 2565).

## Degree Requirements (Computer Engineering International)

- **Total credits:** 144
- **Duration:** 4-year full-time, max 8 years
- **Language:** English throughout

### Credit Structure

| Group | Credits |
|-------|---------|
| General Education (GE) | 30 |
| — Basic Requirements (Charm School, DIQ) | 6 |
| — Language & Communication | 3 |
| — Faculty Requirement | 9 |
| — GE Electives | 12 |
| Specific Courses | 108 |
| — Core Courses | 90 |
| — Electives in Computer Engineering | 12 |
| — Alternative Study | 6 |
| Free Electives | 6 |
| **Total** | **144** |

## Registration

- Regular semester: **9–22 credits**
- Summer semester: max **9 credits**
- Late registration: within 3 weeks of regular semester start (1 week for summer) — fine applies
- Must pass prerequisites before enrolling in dependent courses

| B+ | 3.5 |
| B | 3.0 |

| C | 2.0 |
| D+ | 1.5 |
| D | 1.0 |
| F | 0 |
| I | Incomplete |
| S/U | Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory |
| T | Transfer |

## Academic Standing

- **Probation:** Cumulative GPA < 2.00
- **Dismissal triggers:**
  - Cumulative GPA < 1.00
  - GPA < 2.00 for two consecutive semesters while on probation
  - Exceeds 8-year limit
  - Caught cheating on exams more than once

## Graduation Requirements

- Complete all required credits with cumulative **GPA ≥ 2.00**
- Pass the **English Exit Exam**
- No outstanding debts/liabilities to institute

### Honors

| Honor | GPA | Conditions |
|-------|-----|------------|
| First Class Honors | ≥ 3.50 | No F/U grades, finish in 4 years |
| Second Class Honors | ≥ 3.25 | No F/U grades, finish in 4 years |

## Disciplinary Rules

- Dress code: university uniform required
- Smoking only in designated areas
- Alcohol and illegal drugs strictly prohibited on campus
- Serious misconduct: exam cheating, forgery, gambling, property damage, plagiarism
- Penalties range from verbal warning → probation → suspension → **expulsion**

## International Program Specific Policies

- Each student assigned an **academic advisor** for progress monitoring
- Remedial English provided for weak English students
- Orientation + pre-sessional course for new students
- GE curriculum targets 21st Century Skills: Management/Leadership, Personal/Professional Skills, Communication
- Program committee regularly audits grades and may interview students/instructors
- Graduation and failure rates monitored and reported annually
