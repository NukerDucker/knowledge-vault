# UX/UI Week 5 — POV Statement + HMW ✅ DONE

**Domain:** KMITL Campus Facility Booking System  
**Group:** 2 · Napaul Intharasing (67011178)

## Empathy Map (updated from Wk3)

| Quadrant | Key items |
|----------|-----------|
| **Says** | "Messaged staff, got left on read." / "Why paper form?" / "Room shows booked even after cancellation." |
| **Does** | Waits for LINE reply; goes in person; submits physical form; contacts staff via LINE |
| **Thinks** | Wants centralised ecosystem; transparent booking status online; self-serve room availability |
| **Feels** | Frustrated by limited slots & conflicts; uncertain waiting for approval; paper forms feel outdated |

**Insight:** Students need a transparent reservation process to feel confident throughout the booking journey.

## POV Statement

> A 3rd-year engineering student needs a way to **track booking status in real time** because staff leave LINE messages unread for days, leaving them unable to confirm whether their activity can proceed until it's too late to replicate.

## HMW Questions

1. How Might We make live room availability visible to everyone in one shared place? *(Both)*
2. How Might We notify users automatically at every approval stage so they never need to message staff? *(Both)*
3. How Might We surface estimated approval time upfront so students can plan? *(Student)*
4. How Might We replace LINE as submission channel so messages cannot be left unread? *(Both)*
5. How Might We auto-release a room the moment a booking is cancelled so freed slots are never wasted? *(Both)*

## Top 2 HMWs

**HMW 1 + HMW 2** — all other HMWs subsume into these two (submission channel ⊆ booking status; approval time + auto-release ⊆ live availability + notification).
