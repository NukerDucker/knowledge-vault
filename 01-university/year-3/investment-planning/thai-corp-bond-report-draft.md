**Introductory paragraph:**

The assignment provided six corporate debentures for evaluation. Table 1 presents a side-by-side comparison of all six candidates, benchmarked against the current bank deposit rate of approximately 1.0% (equal to the Bank of Thailand policy rate as of 2026) as the minimum return threshold (Bank of Thailand, 2026; Investment Planning Course, 2026).

**Table 1: Candidate Debenture Screening Overview**

| Issuer | Credit Rating (Agency) | Outlook | Coupon Rate | Tenor | Excess Return vs. Deposit¹ | Default Prob.² | Decision |
|--------|------------------------|---------|-------------|-------|---------------------------|----------------|---------|
| SAWAD (No. 2/2026 Tranche 1) | A-(tha) — Fitch | Stable | 2.95% p.a. | 3 yr | +1.95% | 0.334% | ✅ Selected |
| BTS Group (Jan 2025 2-yr) | BBB+ — TRIS | **Negative** | 4.30% p.a. | 2 yr | +3.30% | 0.582% | ✅ Selected |
| Jaymart Group (JMART26OA) | BBB / BBB− — TRIS | Stable | 4.50% p.a. | 4 yr | +3.50% | — | ❌ Eliminated |
| Thai AirAsia (TAA26NB) | BBB− — TRIS | Stable | Institutional only | 2 yr | — | — | ❌ Eliminated |
| Mudman PCL (MUD) | BB− — TRIS | **Negative** | 7.35% p.a. | 2 yr | +6.35% (nominal) | **2.483%** | ❌ Eliminated |
| MQDC / DTGO | BB+ — TRIS | Stable | 6.80–6.90% p.a. | ~2 yr | +5.80% | — | ❌ Eliminated |

*¹ Excess return = coupon rate − 1.0% (Bank of Thailand policy rate, 2026).*
*² One-year probability of default by credit rating grade. Source: Thai Bond Market Association (ThaiBMA, 2026), as cited in Investment Planning Course (2026).*

**Elimination paragraph:**

Four candidates were eliminated at the screening stage. Mudman PCL (MUD, rated BB−/Negative) and MQDC/DTGO (rated BB+) fall below the minimum investment-grade threshold of BBB−, as classified by TRIS Rating (TRIS Rating, 2026a; 2026b). MUD additionally exhibits active covenant breaches — net debt-to-EBITDA at 4.86x (limit: 3.0x) and debt service coverage ratio at 0.64x (limit: 1.2x) — and reported consecutive net losses totalling 978.45 million Baht across Y/E 2024 and Y/E 2025 (Stock Exchange of Thailand, 2026c). Thai AirAsia's TAA26NB notes were structured for institutional investors only and carry material aviation fuel price and non-cash foreign-exchange risk (TRIS Rating, 2026c). Jaymart Group (JMART) presents a concentrated 2026 refinancing wall of 6,802 million Baht across three maturity tranches, with a current ratio of only 0.62x as of Q1/2026, indicating near-term liquidity stress (JMART, 2026). SAWAD and BTS Group, the two remaining investment-grade retail-accessible candidates, proceed to detailed analysis.

**Summary sentence:**

A principal of 1,000,000 Baht deployed across both debentures and held to maturity returns 1,087,500 Baht gross (1,074,375 Baht net of withholding tax), representing a blended portfolio yield of 3.49% — more than three times the 1.0% available from bank deposits during the same period (Bank of Thailand, 2026).
