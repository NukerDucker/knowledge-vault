## Part 1: Thai Technology Stocks (8 Points)

### Stock A: Advanced Info Service Public Company Limited (ADVANC)

**Basic Information**

Advanced Info Service Public Company Limited (AIS) is Thailand's largest mobile network operator, founded in April 1986 and listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand under the ticker **ADVANC**. The company operates the AIS brand, serving approximately 50 million subscribers across 4G and 5G mobile networks, fixed broadband (AIS Fibre), enterprise digital services, and cloud infrastructure (AIS Cloud, powered by Oracle Cloud Infrastructure). AIS is majority-controlled by Intouch Holdings (INTUCH), which is in turn majority-owned by Temasek Holdings, Singapore's government investment agency.

**Products and Services**
- Mobile telephony: 2G/3G/4G/5G post-paid and pre-paid (1-2-Call brand)
- Fixed broadband: AIS Fibre, Triple T Broadband (3BB) subsidiary
- Enterprise cloud: AIS Cloud (dSURE 3-Star certified — highest Thai cloud accreditation)
- Digital services: AIS Play OTT platform, mPay e-wallet, AIS SUPER WIFI (100,000+ hotspots)

**Index Membership**
- SET50 (top 50 Thai blue-chip stocks)
- SET100
- Thailand Sustainability Index (THSI)

**Internal Factors Affecting Price**
1. **Subscriber base and brand loyalty:** Dominant market position (~50 million users) ensures reliable recurring revenue and strong cash generation.
2. **5G ARPU uplift:** 5G migration drives higher average revenue per user, expanding margins over the 3–4 year horizon.
3. **Service diversification:** Expansion into enterprise cloud (AIS Cloud), IoT, and digital services adds growth revenue streams beyond mature mobile.
4. **Spectrum debt amortization:** Ongoing spectrum license repayment moderates free cash flow but is manageable within existing earnings.

**External Factors Affecting Price**
1. **Market duopoly:** Following TRUE Corporation's merger with DTAC, ADVANC now competes in a two-player market. While competition intensifies on pricing, the duopoly structure limits destructive price wars.
2. **Regulatory environment:** The NBTC governs spectrum auctions and roaming regulations, creating policy uncertainty that can affect cost structure.
3. **Thai macroeconomic conditions:** Thailand's GDP growth directly affects consumer spending on telecom services and enterprise IT budgets.
4. **5G government tailwind:** Thailand's digital economy policy actively supports 5G infrastructure investment, benefiting ADVANC as the leading operator.

**Dividend and Financial Profile**
- Annual dividend yield: approximately 4–6% per year — one of the most consistent dividend payers on SET
- Dividend policy: semi-annual payments; payout ratio 70–90% of net profit
- Annual revenue: ~THB 200+ billion; net profit ~THB 30–35 billion
- EPS growth: moderate and consistent

**Investment Rationale**
ADVANC is selected for consistent dividend income and defensive capital stability. The 4–6% annual dividend yield produces reliable cash returns during the 3–4 year holding period, while 5G adoption and enterprise cloud growth provide moderate capital appreciation upside. Allocation: **150,000 THB (15%)**.

### Stock B: Delta Electronics (Thailand) Public Company Limited (DELTA)

**Basic Information**

Delta Electronics (Thailand) Public Company Limited is a Thai-listed manufacturer of power electronics, EV charging components, and energy management systems. Listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand under the ticker **DELTA**, the company is a subsidiary of Delta Electronics, Inc. (Taiwan, TWSE: 2308), a global leader in power and thermal management with USD 17.9 billion in revenue (2025) and 83,000 employees worldwide. DELTA Thailand operates manufacturing facilities in the Bangpoo Industrial Estate, with over 80% of revenue derived from exports.

**Products and Services**
- Power electronics: switching power supplies, embedded power systems, data center rectifiers and liquid cooling
- EV components: onboard chargers and power conversion units for global automotive OEMs; EV charging station equipment
- Industrial automation: telecom power systems, energy management, building automation
- Data center infrastructure: high-efficiency power supplies for AI hyperscale data centers

**Index Membership**
- SET50
- SET100

**Internal Factors Affecting Price**
1. **EV demand tailwind:** DELTA directly supplies EV onboard chargers and charging infrastructure to major global auto manufacturers. EV market growth is the primary revenue driver.
2. **AI data center expansion:** Growing demand for high-efficiency power management in AI hyperscale data centers creates a strong second growth vector.
3. **Thailand manufacturing advantage:** BOI tax incentives and a skilled workforce provide competitive production costs versus Chinese peers.
4. **R&D and innovation:** Continuous investment in high-efficiency power conversion maintains technological differentiation.

**External Factors Affecting Price**
1. **Global EV cycle risk:** A slowdown in EV adoption or auto production cuts directly reduces order volumes. This is the primary risk.
2. **Currency exposure:** Over 80% of revenue is in USD/EUR while costs are THB-denominated. Thai Baht appreciation compresses margins.
3. **US–China trade diversification benefit:** As OEMs shift manufacturing out of China, DELTA's Southeast Asia base gains order flow.
4. **Competition from Chinese suppliers:** BYD's vertical supply chain and Huawei's power systems compete aggressively on price.

**Dividend and Financial Profile**
- Annual dividend yield: approximately 2–4%
- Dividend policy: regular dividends; earnings reinvestment prioritized for R&D and capacity expansion
- Annual revenue: ~THB 100–120 billion; net profit ~THB 8–12 billion
- EPS growth: accelerating, driven by EV and data center demand

**Investment Rationale**
DELTA provides Thai-listed exposure to two major global secular trends: EV adoption and AI infrastructure. The parent company's global scale and R&D pipeline support multi-year earnings growth. While dividend yield is lower than ADVANC, capital appreciation potential over 3–4 years is higher given EV and data center tailwinds. Allocation: **150,000 THB (15%)**.

## Part 2: Foreign Technology Stocks (8 Points)

### Stock A: NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA)

**Basic Information**

NVIDIA Corporation is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Santa Clara, California, founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem. Listed on NASDAQ (ticker: **NVDA**), NVIDIA pioneered the GPU (Graphics Processing Unit) in 1999 and has since evolved into the world's dominant provider of AI computing infrastructure. Operating a **fabless model**, NVIDIA focuses on chip architecture, software platforms, and AI systems while outsourcing fabrication to TSMC.

**Products and Services**
- **AI/Data Center Hardware:** H100, Blackwell, and Vera Rubin GPU architectures; DGX AI supercomputers; BlueField Data Processing Units (DPUs); Grace and Vera CPUs
- **Software Platforms:** CUDA parallel computing platform (the industry standard for AI model training); Omniverse for 3D simulation and industrial digital twins; TensorRT, DLSS
- **GeForce GPUs:** PC gaming and creator workstation cards
- **Automotive:** DRIVE SoCs and platforms for autonomous vehicles
- **Cloud Services:** GeForce NOW cloud gaming; DGX Cloud AI infrastructure-as-a-service

**Index Membership**
- NASDAQ-100
- S&P 500
- Dow Jones Global Titans 50

**Financial Performance (Revenue and Net Income Growth)**

| Year | Revenue (USD bn) | Net Income (USD bn) | Revenue Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 26.9 | 9.8 | — |
| 2023 | 27.0 | 4.4 | +0.2% |
| 2024 | 60.9 | 29.8 | +125.8% |
| 2025 | 130.5 | 72.9 | +114.2% |
| 2026 | 215.9 | 120.1 | +65.5% |

NVIDIA's revenue surged from USD 27 billion in 2023 to USD 216 billion in 2026 — an 8× increase in three years — driven almost entirely by AI data center GPU demand from hyperscalers (Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta).

**Internal Factors Affecting Price**
1. **AI GPU monopoly:** NVIDIA's H100/Blackwell GPUs have no equivalent at scale. CUDA's deep software ecosystem creates strong customer switching costs — most AI workloads are built on CUDA and cannot easily migrate to competitor hardware.
2. **Data center revenue acceleration:** The data center segment now constitutes over 80% of NVIDIA's revenue, driven by AI training and inference demand.
3. **Blackwell and next-generation roadmap:** The Blackwell GPU architecture and Vera Rubin platforms sustain NVIDIA's performance leadership through 2028.
4. **Customer concentration risk:** Heavy dependence on a small number of hyperscalers means a single customer capex cut can meaningfully impact quarterly results.

**External Factors Affecting Price**
1. **AI infrastructure supercycle:** Hyperscaler AI capital expenditure continues expanding. Microsoft, Amazon, Google, and Meta have collectively committed hundreds of billions in AI infrastructure, most requiring NVIDIA GPUs.
2. **US–China export controls:** US Department of Commerce restrictions limit NVIDIA's ability to sell advanced chips into China, reducing a previously significant market. Any escalation of restrictions is a near-term negative catalyst.
3. **Competition from AMD and custom chips:** AMD's MI-series and Google TPU/Amazon Trainium custom ASICs are growing alternatives, though NVIDIA retains dominant share through CUDA lock-in.
4. **Geopolitical risk via TSMC dependency:** All advanced NVIDIA chips are fabricated by TSMC in Taiwan. Cross-strait tensions represent a tail risk.

**Dividend Policy**
NVIDIA pays a nominal dividend (approximately USD 0.01 per share quarterly), yielding near 0%. This reflects a deliberate capital allocation policy: NVIDIA reinvests operating cash flow into R&D, product development, and share buybacks rather than dividend distributions. For investors focused on maximizing total return over 3–4 years, capital appreciation potential far outweighs the absence of dividend income — consistent with the report's objective of maximizing return for this allocation.

**Investment Rationale**
NVIDIA is the clearest way to invest in the AI infrastructure buildout. Revenue has grown 8× in three years, and the company's CUDA ecosystem creates durable competitive moats. The 30% allocation reflects NVIDIA's role as the highest-conviction growth holding in the portfolio. Allocation: **300,000 THB (30%)**.

### Stock B: Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSMC / TSM)

**Basic Information**

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) is the world's largest dedicated semiconductor foundry, headquartered in Hsinchu Science Park, Taiwan, founded in 1987 by Morris Chang. Listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange (TWSE: 2330) and on the New York Stock Exchange via ADR (ticker: **TSM**), TSMC controls approximately **70% of global semiconductor foundry market share** and serves as the primary chip manufacturer for NVIDIA, Apple, AMD, Qualcomm, Broadcom, and MediaTek.

**Products and Services**
- **Contract wafer fabrication:** Manufactures chips across nodes from mature processes down to leading-edge 3nm, 2nm, and upcoming A13 technologies
- **Advanced packaging:** CoWoS and 3D interposer packaging critical for AI accelerator chips
- **Photomask manufacturing and design enablement:** Full-service semiconductor production support
- **Multi-project wafer (MPW) services:** Prototyping services for fabless chip designers

**Index Membership**
- Taiwan Stock Exchange (TWSE: 2330) — constitutes ~30% of Taiwan's TAIEX index
- NYSE (TSM) via ADR
- S&P Asia 50
- Dow Jones Global Titans 50
- FTSE4Good Index

**Financial Performance (Revenue and Net Income Growth)**

| Year | Revenue (USD bn) | Net Income (USD bn) | Revenue Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 70.6 | 27.8 | −4.2% |
| 2024 | 88.3 | 35.3 | +25.0% |
| 2025 | 121.4 | 54.1 | +37.6% |
| 2026 (TTM) | 141.7 | 70.7 | +35.6% |

TSMC posted record Q2 2026 net income of USD 22.4 billion (+73% YoY) and raised its 2026 full-year revenue growth guidance to **over 40%** in USD terms, citing continued strong AI chip demand.

**Internal Factors Affecting Price**
1. **Process node leadership:** TSMC is the only foundry producing 3nm at volume, with 2nm (GAA nanosheet) production entering ramp in 2025–2026. No competitor can match this at scale.
2. **AI chip demand lock-in:** Nearly all advanced AI chips (NVIDIA Blackwell, Apple silicon, AMD MI-series) require TSMC's leading-edge nodes and CoWoS packaging. TSMC captures value from every AI chip sold.
3. **Capital expenditure expansion:** 2026 capex raised to USD 60–64 billion, reflecting confident demand visibility and commitment to maintaining leadership.
4. **Global fab diversification:** New fabs in Arizona (US), Kumamoto (Japan), and Dresden (Germany) reduce Taiwan concentration risk and access government subsidies.

**External Factors Affecting Price**
1. **AI supercycle demand:** NVIDIA, Apple, AMD, and hyperscalers driving unprecedented foundry order volumes for advanced nodes.
2. **Geopolitical risk:** TSMC's core manufacturing is in Taiwan. Cross-strait political tensions remain a material tail risk, though multi-country expansion systematically mitigates this.
3. **US export restrictions:** TSMC must comply with US sanctions and cannot supply certain advanced nodes to restricted Chinese entities (e.g., Huawei), limiting the addressable Chinese market.
4. **Semiconductor cyclicality:** TSMC's results are sensitive to broader semiconductor demand cycles (smartphones, PCs, automotive). AI demand currently offsets weakness in consumer segments.

**Dividend Policy**
TSMC pays a quarterly cash dividend. As a mature high-growth company, TSMC maintains dividend payments while simultaneously reinvesting heavily in next-generation process technology. The dividend yield is modest (approximately 1–2%) relative to its growth profile. For a return-maximization strategy over 3–4 years, the primary driver is capital appreciation from AI-driven revenue growth, not dividend income.

**Investment Rationale**
TSMC is the "picks and shovels" play on AI: regardless of which AI models or chip designs win long-term, all require TSMC's fabrication. With revenue growing 35–40% annually and 70% foundry market share at leading-edge nodes, TSMC offers a compelling combination of growth and competitive moat. Allocation: **300,000 THB (30%)**.

## Part 3: Alternative Asset — Gold (XAU)

**Basic Information**

Gold (ISO 4217 code: XAU) is a precious metal that has functioned as a store of value, medium of exchange, and investment asset for millennia. Unlike equity investments, gold produces no dividends or earnings — its return is entirely driven by price appreciation. In this portfolio, gold serves as a hedge against inflation, geopolitical risk, and currency devaluation, while also providing speculative return potential given its recent strong upward price trend.

**Investment Vehicles**
Gold can be accessed through: physical bullion (bars and coins), gold ETFs (e.g., SPDR Gold Shares), gold futures and derivatives (traded on COMEX), and gold savings accounts. For Thai investors, domestic gold trading through established dealers (e.g., Gold Traders Association Thailand, spot price in THB/baht) or Gold ETFs listed on SET are practical access points.

**Factors Driving Gold Price Higher**
1. **Inflation and currency devaluation:** Gold is the primary traditional hedge against fiat currency purchasing power erosion. During periods of elevated inflation, investors shift into gold as a store of value.
2. **US Dollar weakness:** Gold is priced in USD globally. When the dollar weakens, gold becomes cheaper for non-USD buyers, lifting demand and price.
3. **Geopolitical risk:** Armed conflicts, political crises, and economic sanctions trigger "flight to safety" capital flows into gold. Israel-Iran tensions drove gold to near two-month highs in June 2025.
4. **Central bank buying:** Central banks hold approximately 19% of all above-ground gold as reserves. China, Russia, and other nations have actively increased gold reserves, providing a large and persistent institutional bid.
5. **Rising US Treasury yields and stubborn inflation:** According to CME Group, these factors, combined with geopolitical risks and robust central bank demand, underpinned gold's surge past USD 4,000/oz for the first time in October 2025 — its largest annual increase (+50%) since the inflationary shock of 1979.

**Expected Price Trend**
Gold broke above USD 4,000 per troy ounce in October 2025 for the first time in history, gaining over 50% in a single year. The drivers underpinning this move — geopolitical uncertainty, USD weakness, inflation persistence, and central bank accumulation — remain intact for the 3–4 year outlook. Historical data confirms gold's cyclical outperformance during periods of macro uncertainty.

**Why Gold Allows Speculation**
Gold's price is driven primarily by macroeconomic sentiment rather than fundamental earnings, creating pronounced volatility and trading opportunity. It is traded continuously across global OTC spot markets (XAU) and derivatives exchanges (COMEX futures, NCDEX India). Leveraged instruments including futures, options, CFDs, and spread betting allow speculative positioning with amplified returns relative to direct bullion ownership.

**Investment Rationale**
Gold at 10% of portfolio provides portfolio-level risk reduction (low/negative correlation with equities), preserves capital during any technology sector drawdown, and offers additional upside from geopolitical and inflationary catalysts. Allocation: **100,000 THB (10%)**.
