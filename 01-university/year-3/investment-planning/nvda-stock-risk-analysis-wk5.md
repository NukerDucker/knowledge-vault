## Analysis of Data

### Risks that move the stock price

1. **Customer concentration.** Large share of revenue from a handful of hyperscalers (Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta). If any cuts AI capex, orders drop fast → price shock. Biggest single risk.
2. **Valuation / expectations risk.** Priced for very high growth. Even a good earnings report can drop the price on a miss vs. the market's high bar. Explains June/July slide (−15.64, then −5.84) after the Apr–May run.
3. **Competition.** AMD (MI-series), custom in-house chips from cloud customers (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium), and cheaper AI-model methods needing fewer GPUs threaten pricing power and margins.
4. **Geopolitical / regulatory.** US–China export controls limit China sales; new restrictions or tariffs cut real revenue overnight. Ongoing overhang.
5. **Supply chain.** Depends on TSMC (Taiwan) for advanced fabrication and on HBM memory supply. Taiwan concentration = geopolitical + single-point risk.
6. **Market / macro risk.** High-beta stock; rate changes and broad tech sell-offs amplify swings both ways.

### Is it a good investment?

Reasonable long-term buy, but volatile.

**For:** Dominant AI-compute position, CUDA software lock-in, strong revenue + margin growth, structural AI demand tailwind.

**Against:** High valuation, big single-day swings (see June/July), customer-concentration and China-policy exposure.

**Verdict:** Good for an investor with high risk tolerance and a long horizon who can hold through 15–25% drawdowns. Not suitable if you need stable, low-volatility returns — the monthly swings above show why. Size the position accordingly; don't over-concentrate.
