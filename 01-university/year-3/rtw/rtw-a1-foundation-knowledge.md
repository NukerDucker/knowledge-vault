## What HR Requires from Job Applicants (10 Skills)

1. Communication skills
2. Problem-solving ability
3. Teamwork / collaboration
4. Adaptability
5. Technical competence
6. Time management
7. Leadership potential
8. Critical thinking
9. Attention to detail
10. Willingness to learn

---

## Hard Skills vs. Soft Skills

**Hard skills** — specific, teachable abilities learned through education or practice.
Examples: programming, data analysis, foreign languages, accounting, graphic design, digital marketing, project management tools.

**Soft skills** — personal traits and interpersonal abilities affecting how you work with others.
Examples: communication, teamwork, problem-solving, adaptability, emotional intelligence, conflict resolution, creativity.

---

## Regulations Fresh Graduates Should Know

1. Labor law basics — minimum wage, working hours, overtime, probation rules
2. Employment contract terms — fixed-term vs. permanent, notice periods, termination
3. Social security / insurance — employer contribution obligations
4. Work permit / visa — required for jobs abroad or foreign workers
5. NDAs / non-compete agreements — read before signing
6. Anti-discrimination laws — age, gender, religion, disability
7. Data privacy — how employers use your personal info
8. Tax obligations — income tax deductions from salary
9. Company probation policies — what happens if you don't pass
10. Right to resign properly — required notice periods

---

## Job Application Skills for New Graduates

1. Resume / CV writing
2. Cover letter tailoring per company/role
3. Interview skills — answers, body language, follow-up etiquette
4. Networking — LinkedIn, alumni networks, job fairs
5. Job search strategies — job boards, company websites, recruiters
6. Understanding job descriptions — matching hard/soft skills
7. Salary negotiation — know your market value
8. Time management — juggling multiple applications
9. Professional communication — emails, follow-ups with HR
10. Personal branding — LinkedIn profile, portfolio
11. Adaptability — adjusting to new company culture
12. Digital literacy — email, spreadsheets, video calls, project tools
