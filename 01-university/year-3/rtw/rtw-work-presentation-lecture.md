# Work Presentation

By Arjarn Krongthip Junyaem

## What Is a Presentation?

A structured way of communicating information, ideas, results, or recommendations to an audience.

> **Good Presentation = Good Content + Good Structure + Good Delivery + Good Visuals**

A good presentation should be: **Clear · Concise · Organized · Engaging · Professional**

## Objectives of a Presentation

- **To Inform** — provide information
- **To Explain** — make something easier to understand
- **To Persuade** — influence opinions or decisions
- **To Report** — present results or progress
- **To Recommend** — suggest ideas or actions
- **To Solve Problems** — propose solutions

Workplace examples: Internship Presentation, Project Presentation, Sales Presentation, Meeting Presentation, Progress Report, Final Project Presentation.

## Know Your Audience

Before preparing, ask: **Who is my audience?**
- Who are they? What do they already know? What do they need to know?
- How much time do they have? What do you want them to do after?

| Audience | Focus |
|---|---|
| Teacher | Ideas, reasons, evidence |
| Manager | Results, problems, solutions |
| Customer | Benefits and value |

## Structure: Opening → Body → Closing

> **Tell them what you are going to tell them. Tell them. Tell them what you told them.**

### Opening (4 parts)
1. **Greeting** — "Good morning, everyone."
2. **Introduce Yourself** — "My name is Narin, and I am a marketing intern."
3. **Introduce the Topic** — "Today, I'm going to talk about our social media campaign."
4. **Give an Overview** — "My presentation is divided into three parts: the objectives, results, and recommendations."

**How to get attention:** question / interesting fact / short story / problem / picture / statistic

### Body
- Approximately **3–5 key points**
- One idea at a time — do not give too much information at once

**Signposting language:**
- Starting: "First, I'd like to talk about…"
- Moving on: "Now, let's move on to…" / "Next, I'd like to explain…"
- Giving examples: "For example…" / "For instance…"
- Highlighting: "The most important point is…"
- Summarizing: "To summarize…"

### Closing (3 parts)
1. **Summary** — "To summarize, there are three main points…"
2. **Conclusion / Recommendation** — "Based on these results, I recommend that…"
3. **Thank the Audience** — "Thank you for your attention. I'd be happy to answer any questions."

## Delivery Skills

### Voice Skills
- **Volume** — speak loudly enough
- **Speed** — do not speak too fast
- **Tone** — vary your voice
- **Pause** — pause between important points
- **Emphasis** — stress important words

### Body Language
> **Eye Contact + Smile + Good Posture + Natural Gestures**

- **Eye Contact** — look at the audience, not only the screen
- **Facial Expression** — use appropriate expressions
- **Gestures** — use natural hand movements
- **Posture** — stand straight and confidently
- **Movement** — move naturally and with purpose
