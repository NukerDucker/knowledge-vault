# RTW A2 — Labour Law and Social Insurance Fund

**File:** `~/Documents/University/Year-3/Ready To Work/A2 Labour law and Social Insurance fund-1-2026.pdf`

---

## Employment Relationship

- **Employer** — hires and pays wages
- **Employee** — works in exchange for wages
- Types: permanent, temporary, part-time, full-time, intern

---

## Employment Contract Elements

1. Employer and employee info
2. Duties and responsibilities
3. Salary and benefits
4. Working hours and holidays
5. Leave conditions
6. Termination conditions
7. Additional terms (NDA, confidentiality, etc.)

---

## Working Hours

| Work type | Max/day | Max/week |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| General / commercial | 8 hrs | 48 hrs |
| Industrial | 8 hrs | 48 hrs |
| Hazardous to health | 7 hrs | 42 hrs |

Rest: at least 1 hour after every 5 continuous hours.

---

## Overtime Pay

- **Weekday OT** (after official hours): **1.5× hourly rate**
- **Holiday / weekly day-off OT** (0–8 hrs excl. rest): **3× normal wage**
- Probation employees are also entitled to OT pay.

---

## Leave Policies (Private Sector)

| Leave type | Days | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|
| Annual leave | 6 days | After 1 year; can carry over |
| Sick leave | Actual illness | Paid max 30 days; medical cert if >3 days |
| Personal leave | 3 days | Paid |
| Maternity leave | 98 days | 45 days paid by company + 45 days from SSF |
| Sterilization leave | As ordered by doctor | Paid |
| Military leave | As called by government | Paid max 60 days |
| Training leave | By agreement | Unpaid |

---

## Termination Compensation

| Service duration | Severance (days of current wage) |
|-----------------|----------------------------------|
| 120 days – < 1 year | 30 days |
| 1 – < 3 years | 90 days |
| 3 – < 6 years | 180 days |
| 6 – < 10 years | 240 days |
| 10 – < 20 years | 300 days |
| 20+ years | 400 days |

Applies when termination is without fault (business closure, restructuring, relocation).

---

## Social Security Fund (SSF)

| | Section 33 | Section 39 | Section 40 |
|--|------------|------------|------------|
| Who | Office employees | Ex-S33 members (within 6 months of leaving) | Freelancers |
| Contribution | ~5% salary | 100 THB/month | 70–300 THB/month |
| Benefits | 7 cases | 6 cases | 3–5 cases |

**Benefits covered:** Accident/illness, childbirth, disability, death, child support, old age, unemployment (S33 only).
