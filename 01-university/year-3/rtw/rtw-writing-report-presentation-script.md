# RTW — Writing Report Storytelling Presentation Script

**For:** Online class 8 Oct 2026 — present the writing report like a telling story
**Based on:** Case 2 — Late Attendance Report (submitted to MS Teams 24 Sep 2026)
**Delivery time:** ~1.5–2 min

---

Good morning, everyone. I would like to present my writing report, Case 2, the Late Attendance Report.

---

So, imagine you are an HR Officer at SiamPro Manufacturing. One day in early September, the Production Manager walks into your office and says, "Something is wrong. Every morning this August, my line started late." That is where my report begins.

My name in the report is Napaul Intharasing, HR Officer at SiamPro Manufacturing. My job that month was to find out what was really happening — not just who was late, but why.

We went through the attendance records of all 30 employees in the Production Department. And what we found was this: 12 out of 30 people — that is 40% — arrived late at least once during August. In total, there were 35 late arrivals recorded. Because of that, the production line was delayed by 10 to 15 minutes almost every morning. That sounds small, but over a whole month, it adds up.

So we did not just look at numbers. We sat down with the employees and talked to them. They told us the real reasons. Traffic congestion in the morning. Long distances from home to the factory. The shuttle bus kept breaking down and there was no backup. And for employees on the early shift, it was simply very hard to wake up in time.

After gathering all of this, we took the first steps: HR reminded those with repeated late arrivals, and we formally notified the Production Manager about the patterns we found.

But the bigger question was: how do we fix this? Our recommendations were three. First, arrange an earlier morning shuttle so workers can beat the traffic. Second, give employees who live far away a flexible start option. Third, check attendance records every month — not just when a problem becomes serious.

In conclusion, the late arrivals in August were not about laziness or attitude. They came from a transportation problem that the company had not yet solved. With better shuttle arrangements and some scheduling flexibility, we believe attendance will improve.

Thank you.

---

## Delivery Notes

- Pause after "That is where my report begins."
- Slow down at numbers — 12 out of 30, 35 arrivals, 10–15 minutes
- Eye contact on "They told us the real reasons" — emotional center of the story
- Do not rush recommendations, one breath per point
