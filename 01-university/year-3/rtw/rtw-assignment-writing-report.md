**Report on Employee Late Attendance**

Prepared by: Napaul Intharasing
Position: Human Resources Officer
Department: Human Resources Department
Company: SiamPro Manufacturing Co., Ltd.
Reporting Period: August 2026
Date Submitted: 24 September 2026

### 1. Objective

This report aims to identify the causes of late attendance among Production Department employees in August 2026 and recommend actions to prevent recurrence.

### 2. Background

The Human Resources Department reviewed attendance records after the Production Manager reported repeated delays at shift start times throughout August 2026.

### 3. Activities Completed

During this reporting period, the HR Department:

- Reviewed attendance records for all 30 Production Department employees
- Conducted individual interviews to identify reasons for late arrivals
- Compiled findings and prepared this report for management review

### 4. Results

The review found that 12 out of 30 employees (40%) arrived late at least once, with 35 late arrivals recorded in total. As a result, the production line started 10 to 15 minutes late on multiple occasions, reducing daily output.

### 5. Problems / Challenges

The main causes reported by employees included heavy traffic congestion, long commuting distances, shuttle bus breakdowns with no backup schedule, and oversleeping due to early shift start times.

### 6. Actions Taken

The HR Department issued verbal reminders to employees with repeated late arrivals and notified the Production Manager of the attendance patterns identified in this review.

### 7. Recommendations

- Arrange an earlier morning shuttle to reduce the impact of peak traffic
- Introduce flexible start times for employees with long commuting distances
- Review attendance records monthly to detect patterns early

### 8. Next Steps

During the next reporting period, the department will present these recommendations to senior management, monitor attendance records weekly, and follow up with employees on available transportation arrangements.

### 9. Conclusion

Late arrivals in August 2026 stemmed mainly from transportation and commuting difficulties. With revised shuttle arrangements and flexible scheduling, the department expects attendance to improve in the following month.
