## A3 Knowledge — Organization Culture

### Definition

> Organizational culture is "the shared values, beliefs, behaviors, and ways of working that guide how employees interact and perform within an organization."

### Components of Corporate Culture

| Component | Examples |
|-----------|---------|
| Values | Integrity, Innovation, Respect, Customer Focus, Teamwork |
| Behaviors | Punctuality, helping coworkers, sharing ideas, taking responsibility |
| Communication | Formal, informal, open-door, hierarchical |
| Leadership style | Coaching, participative, authoritative, democratic |
| Work environment | Flexible, hybrid, office-based, remote |

### The Four Culture Types (Competing Values Framework)

| Culture | Characteristics | Examples | Strengths | Challenges |
|---------|----------------|---------|-----------|------------|
| **Clan** | Family atmosphere, teamwork, mentoring, collaboration | Small businesses, family companies, non-profits | High engagement, strong teamwork | Slow decisions, avoids hard conversations |
| **Adhocracy** | Innovation, risk-taking, creativity, entrepreneurial | Startups, tech firms, research orgs | Creative thinking, fast innovation | High uncertainty, frequent change |
| **Market** | Competition, performance targets, results-driven | Sales companies, consulting, finance | High productivity, goal-oriented | High pressure, stressful |
| **Hierarchy** | Rules, stability, procedures, compliance | Government, banks, manufacturing | Consistency, risk control | Less flexible, slower decisions |

### What Millennials & Gen Z Want

1. Flexibility — remote/hybrid work
2. Purpose-driven work — meaningful contribution
3. Open communication — transparency and honest feedback
4. Diversity and inclusion
5. Opportunities for growth — continuous learning
6. Work-life balance
7. Innovation-friendly — support for new ideas

## A3 Case Studies Summary

| Case | Company | Culture | Key Clues |
|------|---------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | ABC Technology | **Adhocracy** | Casual dress, first names with CEO, WFH 2 days, experimentation encouraged |
| 2 | XYZ Sales Company | **Market** | Leaderboards, performance bonuses, Employee of Month, promotion by results |
| 3 | Sunshine Foods | **Clan** | Birthday celebrations, team lunches, mentoring, managers know everyone's name |
| 4 | Government office | **Hierarchy** | Uniforms, detailed procedures, approval required, formal reporting |
| 5 | Sarah's 4 offers | A=Clan, B=Adhocracy, C=Market, D=Hierarchy | Choose by personality fit |
| 6 | Culture Clash (David) | Bank=Hierarchy → Startup=Adhocracy | Struggled: preferred clear rules; adaptability is key skill |

**Key lesson:** No single "best" culture. Fit your values and working style to the culture type.
