## Issues Requiring Correction

1. **Fatal:** Fix ผู้เช่า name — body vs. signature block mismatch. Attach ID card copy.
2. **Fatal:** Delete Art 11 engine/gear oil language — MG EP Plus has no engine. Define actual EV service schedule (10,000 km: brake fluid, tires, cabin filter, software update).
3. **Payment cycle contradiction:** Art 2 says "daily," Art 3 says "every 3 days." Choose one; ambiguity construed against drafter.
4. **Authorize intended use:** If ride-hailing/Grab, write it into Art 9. Otherwise renter is in breach from day 1.
5. **Add insurance clause:** State voluntary insurance policy exists, class, named driver, commercial use coverage.
6. **Add deposit-return terms:** Timeline and conditions for return at contract end.
7. **Cap Art 5 downtime penalty:** Set maximum days; add rent suspension during repair days renter can't use the car.
8. **Add traffic fine pass-through:** Camera fines during rental period passed to renter.
9. **Attach condition report:** Dated photos + odometer at handover, initialled by both parties.
10. **Add mileage/geographic limits** if lessor wants them.
11. **No early exit clause:** 15-month lock-in with no renter termination right. Consider adding.

## Overall Balance

**Strongly lessor-favored.** Every liability clause runs one way — renter absorbs all damage, theft, injury, owner's taxes and insurance, and warranty risk. Lessor carries no stated obligations beyond delivering the car.

The contract is structurally weak for the lessor in practice: undersized deposit, partly deferred, wrong renter name, contradictory payment terms, and penalty amounts a court can cut down under §383.

**Neither party is well-served by this draft as written.**
